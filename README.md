# FUTURE_DS_01 – Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 1**.

The objective of this project is to analyze business sales data and identify important patterns in **revenue trends, product performance, category performance, profitability, and regional sales**.

An interactive **Business Sales Performance Dashboard** was created using Microsoft Excel to present the analysis and generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Identifying revenue trends over time
- Finding the top-selling products
- Comparing sales and profit across categories
- Analyzing regional sales performance
- Calculating key business KPIs
- Generating actionable business recommendations

---

- Data Cleaning

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Revenue Trends Over Time

Sales were analyzed across different years to understand revenue performance and identify the strongest-performing year.

**Key Finding:**

**2017** recorded the highest annual sales, generating approximately **733,215.26** in revenue.

---

### 2. Top-Selling Products

Product-level sales performance was analyzed to identify the products generating the highest revenue.

**Top-Selling Product:**

**Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier**

- Sales: **61,599.82**

This product recorded the highest sales among the products analyzed.

---

### 3. Category Sales & Profit Performance

Sales and profit were compared across different product categories to identify high-value business areas.

**Highest-Performing Category: Technology**

- Sales: **836,154.03**
- Profit: **145,454.95**

Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the analyzed categories.

---

### 4. Regional Performance

Sales performance was analyzed across the different business regions.

**Highest-Sales Region: West**

- Sales: **725,457.82**

The West region recorded the highest regional sales.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- The **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the top-selling product, generating approximately **61,599.82** in sales.
- **Technology** was the highest-performing category, generating **836,154.03** in sales and **145,454.95** in profit.
- **2017** recorded the highest annual sales, with approximately **733,215.26** in revenue.
- The **West** region generated the highest regional sales, with approximately **725,457.82** in sales.
- The dashboard combines sales, profit, product, category, time, and regional analysis to provide a consolidated view of business performance.

---

## 📌 Actionable Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business actions can be considered:

1. **Increase inventory and marketing focus** on the top-selling Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier to support continued demand.

2. **Continue investing in the Technology category** and promote profitable products to maintain strong sales and profit performance.

3. **Analyze the successful sales strategies from 2017** and identify approaches that can be applied to improve future sales performance.

4. **Strengthen sales and marketing efforts in the West region** while studying its successful performance for strategies that could be applied to other regions.
